# Phase 4 CRM contract

Design recorded before implementation. CRM is an organization-scoped sales workflow over existing Customers, Projects and Quotations. New leads are pre-customer opportunities. Conversion links existing masters or creates them atomically; it never copies activity history into a second customer database.

Pipeline stages have configurable labels and stable open/won/lost semantics. Lead lifecycle is open/won/lost/archived; conversion is a separate link, not a terminal stage. Lost requires an active configured reason. Manual Won requires lead.win and a reason; issuing a quotation does not win a lead. Customer approval establishes commercial acceptance and makes the lead eligible for an explicit Won action. Customer decline does not automatically lose the lead.

Sources, stages, lost reasons and requirement fields are tenant configuration. Interior starter definitions belong to the industry pack. Requirements are bounded typed key/value answers validated against that configuration, never Interior columns in the lead table.

Mutations serialize on the existing organization lock, check optimistic versions, current membership, permissions and CRM entitlement, and write audit records in the same transaction. Cross-tenant composite foreign keys cover parents and assignments. Suspended users cannot receive assignments. Initial CRM screens require organization-wide grants, matching Phase 3; branch filtering is not a permission bypass.

Manual activities are append-only. System events are generated by database operations and cannot be impersonated by manual note commands. Follow-up completion is idempotent; the lead's next follow-up derives from pending tasks. Site visits retain the lead link after conversion. Dashboard attention queues replace a separate notification subsystem. Lists, selectors and timelines are paginated server-side.

Phone comparison preserves an explicit international + prefix (00 is normalized to +), strips visual separators, and never invents a country calling code. National numbers remain national comparison forms; cross-country equivalence is not inferred. Duplicate matches are tenant-local warnings, never automatic merges. Attachments remain deferred until private Storage policies and live storage acceptance exist.

## Screens and setup

Open /dashboard/crm for attention queues, /leads for a paginated list, /pipeline for the board, /followups and /site-visits for task queues, /commercial for customer-response queues, and /settings for tenant configuration. Lead detail includes manual notes, reassignment, stage changes, requirements, follow-up completion and visit recording. Desktop drag/drop and a native stage selector call the same checked server action. Lost/Won transitions use the workspace to capture their required reason.

Apply seed-crm.sql after development identity, catalog and commercial seeds for a minimal Rahul Residence example. Starter sources, stages and requirements come from packages/industry-interior/src/crm-starter.json. Deactivation preserves historical references. Lists and selector pages are bounded to 25 records; search and page controls retrieve further choices. Phone matching normalizes formatting without guessing country codes; local and international formats are not automatically equated.

CRM and sharing use existing audit storage. Lead creation/edit/assignment/stage/conversion/loss, follow-up creation/completion, visits, share creation/revocation and first customer view/response are recorded transactionally. Activities distinguish manually recorded communications from system events. No email, WhatsApp, push delivery, attachment uploads or calendar integration is simulated.
