# Tax architecture

Tax codes are organization-owned ordered components with names and exact decimal percentage rates. Aggregate configured rate is limited to 100%. Invoice lines may select a code, or the document may apply one code to the aggregate post-discount amount. The chosen components and calculated amounts are snapshotted at invoice save/issue.

For exclusive tax, taxable basis is the post-discount amount. For inclusive tax, the unrounded basis is gross amount divided by one plus aggregate rate. Each component is rounded half-up at contract precision using PostgreSQL numeric. Exclusive total is basis plus the sum of rounded components; inclusive taxable amount is gross less that sum. Document-level calculation rounds once per component, while line-level calculation rounds each line's components. Consequently the modes can produce different totals; the UI explicitly selects the mode and scope.

Decimal text crosses JSON and rendering boundaries. Browser floating-point arithmetic is not used for authoritative totals. Tests cover inclusive/exclusive arithmetic, split components, line/document modes and frozen configuration after issue.

Optional HSN/SAC, billing state, place of supply and business/customer registration identifiers are display metadata. The demo seed provides CGST+SGST and IGST examples only. The system does not infer jurisdiction, validate GSTIN, choose place-of-supply rules, file returns, issue statutory e-invoices or guarantee Indian GST compliance. Tax-code configuration must reflect the business's applicable requirements. No tax is inferred for a receipt or a change-order delta.

## Standalone seller / buyer GST-preparation ledger

The company billing workflow at `/dashboard/billing` is separate from the existing contract-based finance module. Each company (including a marketplace seller) can manage issued sales invoices, supplier purchase-bill register records and manually recorded payments without requiring a project contract.

Company billing settings capture the legal/trade name, business address, GSTIN (if registered), two-digit state code and 2–6 character invoice prefix. Issue-time sales invoice numbering uses a serialized financial-year counter, yielding up to 16-character numbers; issued documents and line snapshots are immutable. Supplier bills retain the supplier's reference, and duplicates for the same supplier GSTIN, financial year and reference are rejected. Every data operation is tenant-isolated, checks the existing `billing` entitlement and organization permissions and is recorded in audit logs.

Invoice lines capture description, HSN/SAC, quantity, unit, rate and manually selected GST percentage. Values are computed in PostgreSQL numeric. The simple Indian GST calculator splits intra-state tax into CGST/SGST and inter-state tax into IGST based on the supplied place-of-supply state and supplier state. No GST rate or place of supply is automatically determined. Taxable-value discounts, cess, composition/exempt invoices, exports/SEZ, reverse-charge sales issuance and detailed cross-border rules are not supported in this workflow. Do not use it to issue invoices for those scenarios without a compliant extension.

The **purchase-bill register is an internal accounting copy, not a replacement for an invoice legally issued by a supplier**. The print/save-as-PDF action creates a browser-printed view, not a digitally signed tax document. It does not verify tax registration, produce IRN/QR codes, submit e-invoices or GSTR returns, reconcile Input Tax Credit, link bank feeds, collect money or contact counterparties. Review India's current invoice rules, signature requirements and the business's e-invoice obligations with a qualified GST professional before treating documents as compliant. Credit notes and electronic reconciliation are future enhancements.
