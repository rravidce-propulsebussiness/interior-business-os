# Execution estimation: Phase 6

Implemented with local verification; see PHASE6-VERIFICATION.md for evidence and hosted acceptance limits. Finished-work quotation pricing remains unchanged. Execution estimation reads only accepted contract scope and approved changes, and never writes customer contract amounts, quotations, invoices or payments. Projects remains the same shared entity.

An estimate root belongs to a contract and has independently numbered revisions. Drafts may be edited, submitted, approved or cancelled; approving a successor supersedes the previous approved revision without changing its content. A new revision copies unchanged scope and estimates, marks removed scope, and adds unestimated approved additions/modifications. Coverage compares current approved commercial scope with the revision; stale scope must be synchronized before approval. Each active scope needs estimated lines or an explicit exclusion reason. Price-only adjustments do not invent physical work.

Materials and variants form an independent purchase/consumption master. A variant has generic attributes, explicit unit-conversion revisions, purchase increments and effective-dated cost sources. Brands are data. Labour, external services, transport and other cost lines do not require material records. Recipes are reusable estimates, not manufacturing BOMs.

Authoritative estimation arithmetic runs in one bounded PostgreSQL numeric function, shared by preview and persistence. Safe rules choose a fixed quantity or one named basis (finished quantity/area, dimension or volume), multiplied by a decimal factor. No executable expression text is accepted. Inputs and outputs use decimal strings. Waste precedence is explicit estimate override, recipe value, then material default. The calculation retains base, waste-adjusted consumption, unrounded purchase quantity, rounded purchase quantity and optional reasoned override. Purchase rounding uses an explicit increment after conversion; no implicit cross-material/unit conversion occurs. Preview is advisory and saving repeats validation from current stored configuration.

Cost rows are separate from quantity/scope rows, including estimate costs and PO costs. A quantity-only reader cannot retrieve prices through direct RLS reads, RPCs, documents or audit metadata. Unknown costs remain unknown, not zero. Historical revisions snapshot conversions, recipe rules, material labels, rate source and effective dates. Quantity overrides require an independent grant and reason and append an audit event.

Entitlements: Projects gates estimation/materials; Vendors gates vendor configuration; Purchasing plus Projects gates requisitions/RFQs/POs/receipts. Vendor quote actions also need Vendors. Billing, Website and Quotation are not prerequisites for internal execution estimation once an accepted contract exists. All action grants remain independent; organization-wide workflows deny branch-only grants.

Procurement starts from an approved estimate. A requisition selects quantities from source lines and consolidates compatible variant/unit/service demand while retaining every source allocation. Approval and downstream issue recheck available demand under the organization lock. RFQs solicit several vendors without multiplying demand. Vendor quotes are manually recorded with validity, tax, freight, lead time and terms. Factual comparison has no hidden ranking or automatic award. Users split award by vendor and item, including partial quantities.

Issued POs freeze supplier, business, line prices, tax, allocated freight, delivery and terms. Drafts do not reserve demand; issue does, with aggregate over-order protection. Unreceived orders may be cancelled with a reason; issued content is never rewritten. Partial goods/service receipts are append-only and idempotent. Organization locking protects cumulative received quantities. Received equals accepted plus rejected; default over-receipt is blocked. Rejected quantities do not create inventory or automatically create replacement orders.

Cost terminology is explicit: estimated execution cost, committed PO cost, and value of accepted receipts. Actual accounted cost, consumed quantity and realized profit do not exist in this phase. Cost reports group contracts within the selected project and break estimated costs down by cost kind, keeping contract currencies separate. Scope and line screens retain area and classification evidence. Receipt valuation allocates snapshotted PO line costs proportionally to accepted quantities; it is not a vendor bill or accounting expense.

Private storage is not configured, so drawing and supplier-document references are text references only. No fake upload, external email, vendor portal, inventory ledger, stock transfer, sheet nesting, site scheduling, payroll or accounting integration is introduced. Local verification uses PostgreSQL/Auth shims; hosted preflight found no credentials and hosted acceptance remains outstanding.

## Business workflow

Open Project -> Execution, select the accepted contract and create its draft estimate. Scope shows source type, area and coverage. Open a scope to add a material, service or safe rule, or apply a whole recipe. Saved requirements show base, waste, calculated, rounded and planned quantities. Cost-authorized users see the snapshotted rate and source. Saving quantity or note changes preserves that rate unless an explicit replacement is requested.

Submit only after every active scope is estimated or excluded with a reason. Review can return the draft or approve it. Approval freezes the internal document. Create Revision copies the prior scope, lines and costs; approving it supersedes the predecessor. Requisition creation shows required, reserved, ordered and remaining quantities. History remains read-only.

The builder recalculates on save. Master selectors are bounded to 250 choices; document item selectors retrieve all pages up to the supported bound. List/detail tables page in sets of 25. Hosted authenticated interaction has an opt-in Playwright workflow and still requires deployment-specific acceptance.
