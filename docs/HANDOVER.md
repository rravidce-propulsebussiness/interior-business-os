# Handover: Phase 7

Handover snapshots a configured checklist. Approval requires finished project tasks, resolved pre-handover snags, no open critical snag, current passed mandatory/final inspections and explicit material/commercial review notes. A plan must be in handover preparation. Approval freezes the customer-facing snapshot, marks the physical plan handed over and records who approved it. Completion is a subsequent physical transition; it does not close the commercial contract.

Readiness and commercial review appear on `/dashboard/operations/closure`. Forms support handover preparation, checklist results, warranty dates, acknowledgement and approval. Customer-facing PDFs exclude internal review notes and costs; an internal completion report is separate. Local tests cover historical protection, customer projection and competing approvals. Acknowledgement is staff-entered evidence, not a customer signature or portal. Hosted acceptance remains pending.
